# AURA — Public site polish backlog

> **Status (2026-10-13):** Parked — to pick up after branding/logo. Mobile redesign comes after the web work.
> Each item lists what changes and what it does for visitors.

## 1. Home hero
- **Change:** show the featured wallpaper's title and its size/resolution (e.g. "Desktop · 3840 × 2160 · 4K"),
  with **Download** and **View wallpaper** buttons; replace the plain slide dashes with small preview thumbnails
  of the other featured wallpapers.
- **Does:** turns the hero from a passive slideshow into something people can act on — one click to download or
  open the featured wallpaper, and they can see/jump to the other featured picks.

## 2. Section headers + leftover developer text
- **Change:** replace the "EXPLORE / Dive into…" header with sentence-case section titles plus "See all →"
  (e.g. "Trending this week" with All / Phone / Desktop tabs); remove lines like
  "20 wallpapers loaded — more available".
- **Does:** the home page reads like curated sections instead of one endless feed, and nothing on the page looks
  like debug output.

## 3. Category pages
- **Change:** a cover banner using the category thumbnail, the number of wallpapers, the description, and a proper
  breadcrumb (Explore › Category) instead of "← Back"; optionally "related categories" chips.
- **Does:** category pages feel like destinations (better for SEO and sharing), visitors see how much is inside,
  and can hop to similar categories.

## 4. Wallpaper card hover + one card style
- **Change:** on hover show the title, a 4K/resolution badge and a one-click download button (like + save already
  exist); a slight lift + shadow; one corner radius (~14px) and one hover style across home, Latest, Trending,
  categories, search and profile.
- **Does:** faster browsing (download without opening the page) and a consistent, more premium feel everywhere.

## 5. Mobile redesign (after the web is complete)
- **Change:** bottom tab bar (Home · Explore · Trending · Saved · Profile) instead of the hamburger; category rail
  with round thumbnails on the home screen; an Explore tab with grouped picture tiles
  (Places & nature / Art & style / Interests); the Phone/Desktop slider on lists.
- **Does:** one-thumb navigation like a native app, categories visible without opening a menu, and the main pages
  always one tap away.
